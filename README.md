# CAFPT Study Hub

A single-file, offline-capable study hub for the TÜV SÜD Certified Automotive Foundation Level Penetration Tester (CAFPT) course.

- **Flash cards** – every topic (Modules 1 to 7 and General Scope)
- **Cheat sheets** – every topic
- **Practice MCQs** – every topic (430 questions)

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies. Progress is stored in your browser's localStorage.

> Study aid only. Verify details against the official TÜV SÜD course material and the primary standards (ISO/SAE 21434, UN R155/R156).
